\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

% Research area and relevance
Representing three-dimensional shapes is a fundamental challenge in graphics, robotics, and medical imaging. Rather than storing a grid of samples, a neural network can learn a continuous function that determines whether any point lies inside or outside an object, making memory cost independent of resolution. Standard networks struggle to capture sharp detail, however, a limitation that has produced many specialized architectures and little guidance on which to choose.

% Aim and methods
This thesis evaluates eight such architectures under an identical training protocol on the Nefertiti bust, a cultural heritage scan combining smooth facial geometry with fine ornamental detail. Each method is run over four random seeds and compared by intersection-over-union on held-out points --- with error bars and a significance test --- alongside Chamfer distance and normal consistency, and we test whether architectural properties proposed in a recent survey predict the differences observed.

% Results
Two methods form a distinct top tier: Fourier Features and INCODE are statistically indistinguishable from each other but significantly ahead of the rest. A single run had instead suggested a six-way near-tie with a different leader, so the seeds were what separated signal from noise. Yet architecture is not the largest lever: a controlled ablation on one method's frequency scale moved accuracy by 16.7 points --- an order of magnitude more than the gap between the best tier and the next.

% Conclusion
Two conclusions follow. There is a genuine best tier for this task, but configuration can outweigh the choice of architecture by an order of magnitude, so tuning a capable method matters more than selecting among capable methods. And the survey's architectural properties do not predict the winners: the best tier pairs one method with frequency compactness and one without, and a grouped comparison of the property flips sign when a single method is removed. The implementation, dataset, and analysis scripts are released in full.
